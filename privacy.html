1. The short version

  • My apps and games are built to work without an account and to keep what you create on your own device.
  • I do not sell personal information, and I do not share it for advertising.
  • If a specific app does anything more than the baseline described here, it is listed by name in section 4 before that version is released.
  • You can ask me what I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, at the address in section 12.

2. What this covers

I am an independent developer publishing under the name JonT. This policy applies to every app, game and website I publish under that name, on Google Play, the Apple App Store, the web, or anywhere else, including ones released after the effective date above.

It describes a baseline that all of them follow. Section 4 lists each app that differs from the baseline and says exactly how.

3. What my apps handle

Unless section 4 says otherwise for a specific app, this is everything an app of mine touches:

  • What you create in the app. Journal entries, settings, saved games, scores and progress are stored on your device. They are not sent to me, and I cannot see them.
  • Device permissions. An app asks for a permission only when a feature needs it, such as notifications for reminders. You can refuse or revoke any permission in your device settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.
  • Purchases. If an app is paid or offers in-app purchases, the app store handles the payment. I receive confirmation that a purchase happened. I never receive your card number or billing details.
  • Store statistics. Google and Apple give developers aggregate figures such as install counts and crash reports. These arrive under the stores' own privacy policies and do not identify you to me.
  • Messages you send me. If you email me, I receive your address and whatever you write, and I use them only to reply.

By default my apps have no accounts, no advertising, no analytics software, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

5. Other companies

The store you download from (Google Play or the Apple App Store) and your device's operating system process data under their own policies. That happens whether or not my app is involved, and I do not control it.

A future app may need an outside service to do its job. Examples are fetching live content, syncing between devices, showing ads in a free game, or measuring crashes. If an app uses a service like that, section 4 will name the service, say what it receives, and link to its privacy policy before that version is released. I will only use services that commit to protecting your data at least as well as this policy does.

6. Keeping and deleting

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it. Because I never receive it, I have no copy to delete or restore.
  • Emails are kept only as long as it takes to resolve your request, then deleted.
  • Accounts. None of my apps has accounts today. If one ever does, it will let you delete the account and its data from inside the app and from a link on this site.

7. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by the operating system's app sandbox, which keeps other apps from reading it. Anything an app sends over the internet is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No method is perfect, so I keep the amount of data my apps handle as small as I can.

8. Children

My apps are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent me personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California give you the right to see, correct, delete or get a copy of personal information held about you, and to object to how it is used. Whatever your location, you can ask me for any of these at the address in section 12, and I will answer within 30 days.

I do not sell personal information and I do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

10. This website

This site is a static page. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. Its typefaces load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page opens. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub keeps standard server logs that include visitor IP addresses.

11. Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date at the top changes with it. If a change affects how an existing app handles your data, I will describe it here before the updated version of that app is released.
